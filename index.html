James Meese

School of Media & Communication, RMIT University
Co-lead, Diversity, News and AI Lab · Melbourne, Australia

I study how media institutions adapt to digital infrastructures, with a focus on personalisation, recommendation, and the public value of news. I also co-lead the Diversity, News and AI Lab with Prof Haiqing Yu. Our cross-disciplinary team examines how AI shapes news distribution, user engagement, and democratic discourse. We focus especially on culturally and linguistically diverse populations.

Current projects

  • Civic Recommenders — establishing new methods for normative scoring and civic signal modelling.
  • Journalism and AI — exploring the deployment and use of AI in the news media sector.
  • 5G and Telecommunications — understanding deployment and adoption pathways.
